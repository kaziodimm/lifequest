# Product Decisions

## Current Direction

Habidoo remains the product brand.

The core mechanic is now a Life Tree / Life Strategy system, not a classic habit tracker.

The earlier product direction is still valuable and should be preserved:

- global product ambition
- Czech-first option
- multilingual architecture
- five visual styles
- strong custom visual identity
- premium mobile-first app experience
- future Supabase, Vercel, Resend, analytics, ads, and mobile monetization

## Important Product Decision

Habidoo should not copy the prompt literally.

The prompt defines the idea:

> real life as a strategy game, with a technology tree as the core product.

The implementation should adapt this idea into a unique product with its own design language, business model, and user experience.

## Core Product Model

The Life Tree is the main product.

Daily missions, habits, notes, streaks, XP, statistics, and profile all support the Life Tree.

Users should not feel that they are managing todos.

Users should feel that they are developing their real life.

## Visual Direction

Keep the five-theme direction:

1. Focus Dark
2. Soft Light
3. Pixel Quest
4. Cyber Calm
5. Nature Progress

For the new Life Tree concept, these become visual styles for the same strategy interface, not separate products.

## Tone

The app should be:

- strategic
- motivating
- beautiful
- slightly playful
- premium
- not childish
- not corporate
- not a generic task app

## MVP Interpretation

Version 1 should prove one thing:

> Does a Life Tree make personal progress feel more meaningful than a todo list?

If yes, habits, tasks, streaks, mobile apps, ads, premium themes, and social systems can grow around it.
