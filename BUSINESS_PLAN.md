# Habidoo Business Plan

## 1. Product Idea

Habidoo is a mobile-first Life Strategy app that turns real-life development into a premium strategy game.

The user does not simply complete habits or tasks. The user progresses a Life Technology Tree by completing real-world missions that unlock technologies across Health, Mind, Career, Business, Finance, Relationships, and Creativity.

The core promise:

> Develop your real life like a strategy game.

## 2. Target Audience

Initial audience:

- Ambitious adults who like strategy games and self-development.
- Students and young professionals building structure.
- Freelancers, founders, and remote workers.
- People who struggle with morning motivation and decision fatigue.
- Users who like Civilization, Stellaris, RPG progression, Duolingo-style daily loops, and premium mobile apps.

Markets:

- Global from the beginning.
- Czechia and Europe as an early practical market.
- English-speaking market as a main growth path.
- Russian and Ukrainian audiences as strong additional segments.
- Later: app-store distribution worldwide.

## 3. Core Problem

Most productivity apps show users what they failed to do.

Habidoo should show users what their real life is becoming.

The product focuses on this feeling:

> I want my daily actions to feel meaningful, strategic, and connected to a bigger life direction.

## 4. Differentiation

Habidoo is not a habit tracker, task manager, or todo list.

Key differences:

- The Life Tree is the main product.
- Tasks and habits are only fuel for unlocking technologies.
- Progress is shown as strategic development, not checklist completion.
- The interface is inspired by premium strategy games.
- Morning decisions are reduced to a few meaningful missions.
- Multiple visual styles allow the product to appeal to different user segments.

## 5. MVP

Version 1 should include:

- Home screen.
- Life Technology Tree.
- Daily Command Center.
- XP and levels.
- Streaks.
- Basic profile.
- Achievements.
- Statistics.
- localStorage persistence.
- PWA-ready setup.
- Vercel deployment.

No backend in version 1.

Future-ready architecture should prepare for:

- Supabase auth and sync.
- Resend onboarding and reminders.
- Analytics.
- Leaderboards.
- Friends ranking.
- Guilds.
- Mobile ads and premium cosmetics.

## 6. Monetization

The core Life Tree loop should remain free.

Future revenue paths:

- Mobile ads.
- Rewarded ads for boosters, streak protection, and cosmetic unlocks.
- Premium no-ads plan.
- Paid visual themes.
- Cosmetic packs for profile/tree style.
- Supporter upgrade.
- Stripe for web premium only after retention is proven.

Do not monetize before the core return loop is validated.

## 7. Growth Strategy

Primary channel:

- Instagram Reels.
- TikTok.
- YouTube Shorts.

Content angles:

- Turn your life into a strategy game.
- Your real-life technology tree.
- Stop making todo lists. Unlock technologies.
- Morning missions that actually matter.
- XP for building your real life.
- Civilization, but for your personal development.

## 8. Metrics

Track from day one once analytics are added:

- Unique visitors.
- App opens.
- Life Tree opens.
- Mission completions.
- Technology unlocks.
- XP earned.
- Day 1 return.
- Day 7 retention.
- Streak length.
- Theme changes.
- Language selection.

Main MVP metric:

> Does the user return to progress the Life Tree?

## 9. Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation

Product docs, Life Tree concept, architecture, and first visual MVP.

### Phase 2: Web MVP

Build Home, Life Tree, Command Center, Statistics, Profile, XP, streaks, localStorage, and PWA setup.

### Phase 3: Visual Identity

Improve the tree, custom node icons, category visuals, animations, unlock feedback, and the first strong visual style.

### Phase 4: Launch

Deploy to Vercel, connect domain, test on iPhone/Android, and collect feedback.

### Phase 5: Growth

Run short-form video experiments and improve the onboarding/return loop.

### Phase 6: Backend and Accounts

Add Supabase auth, cloud sync, analytics, and Resend emails when the MVP loop is clear.

### Phase 7: Mobile and Revenue

Prepare mobile app distribution, ads, rewarded ads, premium themes, and cosmetics.
